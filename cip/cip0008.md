## Summary
Add a small, readable Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (HMC) module to `mlai`, faithful to Radford Neal's hybrid Monte Carlo formulation, for use in teaching notebooks and lectures (including IEI Week 1's loss-as-potential → kinetic → Hamiltonian arc). This is the first concrete HOW for [REQ-0008](../requirements/req0008_teachable-sampling.md).

## Motivation
Lectures already treat training losses as *energies* and show gradient descent as walking downhill on a potential. The natural next question — *where is the kinetic energy?* — leads to the Hamiltonian $H=K+V$ and to HMC. The `mlai` package has contour and gradient-descent plotting helpers but historically no first-class HMC sampler. Research libraries are poor as a first teaching contact: too much machinery between leapfrog and the student.
